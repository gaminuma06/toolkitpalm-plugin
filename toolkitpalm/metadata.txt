name=ToolkitPalm
qgisMinimumVersion=3.16
description=Detect, count and number oil palm trees, and trim the empty borders of drone orthomosaics.
version=1.2.2
author=Ing. Adán Arias
email=ingarias9006@gmail.com

about=ToolkitPalm brings together geoprocessing tools for oil palm plantations in a single panel, designed to work on drone orthomosaics:
    - Palm Detector: counts and locates every palm tree, with manual editing of the detected points, automatic numbering along planting rows, missing-palm detection and highlighting of off-pattern palms.
    - Trim orthomosaic borders: removes the flat grey, white or black frame around the photographed area.
    .
    All processing runs on a remote server, so no GPU or deep learning libraries are needed on your machine. Large plots are automatically split into blocks, and an interrupted job can be resumed where it stopped.
    .
    Usage requires signing in with a Google account. Detection spends processing credits: one credit covers up to 5 hectares, charged per plot rather than per block. Every new account gets one free trial credit. Credit packages can be purchased from within the plugin.
    .
    The plugin contains no secrets or embedded API keys: authorisation is granted by the user's own Google session, which the server verifies directly with Google.
    .
    Dependencies: requests (shipped with the standard QGIS installation). No additional packages required.
    .
    Spanish: ToolkitPalm reúne herramientas para plantaciones de palma de aceite sobre ortomosaicos de dron: detección, conteo y numeración de palmas, y recorte de los bordes vacíos de la ortofoto. Todo el procesamiento corre en el servidor.
